# Enomoto 2026 aggregate statistics

`ttest_iAUC_1h5h.csv` contains paired t-test summaries supplied by the study author on 2026-08-31 for [Enomoto 2026](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40101-026-00430-0). The statistical attachment is reproduced unchanged.

Each of the four data rows summarizes 13 participants on one intervention day. The contrast is dinner 1 hour minus 5 hours before bedtime. Mean differences, paired-difference SDs, SEs, and confidence limits are in mg/dL × min. The file also reports t statistics, degrees of freedom, and rounded P values. It contains no individual observations, participant identifiers, or correspondence text.

The corresponding extraction rows are O0260–O0263. Day 2 is the primary comparison; Days 1, 3, and 4 are sensitivity analyses. Unit conversion divides concentration-time statistics by 18. Validation checks allow for the rounding in the supplied file.
